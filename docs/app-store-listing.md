### Description (2357/4000)

```text
BTT Dive Planner is a decompression and gas planning tool for scuba divers. It was made for the BTT Explorers Hungary dive club (founded 2018, motto: "Mindig van lejjebb!!!") and is shared with every diver who plans carefully.

The calculation uses the Bühlmann ZH-L16C model with gradient factors (GF low / GF high) and the Schreiner equation. You can choose between a Standard and a more Conservative calculation method.

THREE MODES

Recreational (default): no-decompression dives only. A single 12 or 15 L cylinder, and you must surface with 50 bar. Shows the no-deco limit, the rock bottom reserve and the turn pressure, with a profile chart and a minute-by-minute itinerary.

Technical: decompression dives with GUE standard gases or a PADI/SSI-style gas set. Deco gas switches with a 1-minute hold, minimum gas, backgas and deco gas requirements, and a "What to bring" packing list. In "My gases" mode you enter the cylinders you actually have; the planner checks whether they are enough and tells you exactly how much is missing.

Penetration: cave, mine and wreck dives by GUE, TDI or IANTD rules. Rule of thirds, sixths in siphons, team planning with per-diver gases and cylinder configuration, stage cylinders and deco gases. A solo calculation is available. The "I am brave" override still computes a plan beyond the rules, but clearly flags every violation. If a gas-sharing exit fails, the plan says so plainly: the team does not survive.

Backmount and sidemount configurations are supported, including lost-cylinder checks.

Technical and Penetration modes ask you to confirm your certification once per session.

EXPORT

Create a one-page PDF plan and share it through the iOS share sheet.

PRIVACY AND OFFLINE USE

The app works fully offline. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no network requests. No data is collected, and nothing leaves your device.

Hungarian and English interface, metric and imperial units, light and dark mode.

IMPORTANT

This software is not certified or validated by any diving agency. It is a club tool. Always cross-check your plan against an independent planner.

Every dive carries risk. This app supports you, but it does not replace your own thinking and does not take over the responsibility for planning. Only dive what your training allows – if it doesn't feel right, don't dive!

Made by sadrobot.
```

## 4. Review notes for Apple (English, 1817/4000)

```text
BTT Dive Planner is a decompression and gas planning tool for scuba divers, made for the BTT Explorers Hungary dive club. It calculates dive plans with the Bühlmann ZH-L16C model and gradient factors. It has three modes: Recreational (no-decompression dives, the default), Technical (decompression dives) and Penetration (cave, mine and wreck dives).

No login or account is needed. There is no sign-in screen and no demo account is required.

The app works fully offline. It makes no network requests, contains no analytics or third-party SDKs, and collects no data.

When switching to Technical or Penetration mode, the app shows a one-time (per session) dialog asking the user to confirm that they hold the relevant diving certification. This is a safety measure, not an account or verification step; tapping confirm continues.

The PDF button creates a one-page dive plan and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet (save to Files, AirDrop, Mail, etc.).

The app is a planning aid for trained divers. It shows a safety disclaimer and states that it is not certified by any diving agency.

Suggested test path:
1. Launch the app. It opens in Recreational mode with a default plan; review the no-deco limit, rock bottom reserve, profile chart and itinerary.
2. Change the depth or bottom time and watch the plan update. Optionally switch language (HU/EN), units or light/dark theme in the header.
3. Switch to Technical mode, confirm the certification dialog, and review the deco schedule, gas plan and "What to bring" packing list.
4. Switch to Penetration mode, confirm the certification dialog, and review the team gas plan (rule of thirds, turn pressures).
5. Tap "Save PDF" and confirm that the iOS share sheet opens with the one-page plan. Airplane mode can be enabled at any point; everything keeps working.
```
